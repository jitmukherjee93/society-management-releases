## 📋 How to Install on Android

1. **Download:** Tap the download link above or scan the QR code using your phone's camera.
2. **Accept Security Prompt:** If your browser prompts *"File might be harmful"*, tap **Download anyway** (standard Android prompt for apps downloaded outside Google Play).
3. **Allow Installation:**
   - Tap the completed download notification or open your device's **Files / Downloads** folder.
   - If prompted with *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*, tap **Settings** → toggle **Allow from this source**.
4. **Install & Launch:** Tap **Install**. Once finished, launch the app and log in with your registered phone number / flat credentials.
